## 8. MARKET GAPS AND OPPORTUNITIES

### Gap 1: TikTok and Short-Form Video Content
Only one Canberra cleaning company (Pro Cleaning Services ACT) has an active TikTok. #CleanTok is one of the most popular content categories globally. **Massive opportunity** for before-and-after content, cleaning tips, and satisfying transformation videos.

### Gap 2: Eco-Friendly / Health-Focused Cleaning
While The Greener Cleaner and a few others exist, there is no dominant eco-friendly brand in Canberra. Opportunities include:
- Allergy-safe / medically-focused cleaning (asthma, immunocompromised)
- Verified eco-certifications (beyond generic "green" claims)
- Chemical-free or plant-based product transparency

### Gap 3: NDIS and Aged Care Cleaning
Growing market segment. Only Maid2Match, Absolute Domestics, and a handful of specialists are NDIS-registered cleaners. Demand is increasing as NDIS participant numbers grow.

### Gap 4: App-Based Booking and Technology
Most Canberra cleaners rely on phone calls or basic web forms. There is room for:
- Real-time online booking with instant pricing
- Subscription/membership models
- Real-time cleaner tracking
- Automated quality feedback loops

### Gap 5: Outer Suburb Coverage
Many providers are concentrated in central Canberra. Newer and outer suburbs (Molonglo Valley, Whitlam, Denman Prospect, Taylor, Throsby) are underserved.

### Gap 6: Post-Construction and Builder's Cleaning
Limited specialists for new developments despite Canberra's ongoing construction boom (which is also driving price increases).

### Gap 7: Airbnb / Short-Term Rental Turnovers
With Canberra's growing tourism and events calendar, dedicated Airbnb turnaround cleaning is an underserviced niche.

### Gap 8: Strata and Body Corporate Cleaning
Canberra has a large and growing apartment market. Dedicated strata cleaning specialists are limited beyond New Generation Cleaning and a few commercial operators.

### Gap 9: Content Marketing and SEO Authority
Most local cleaners have thin websites with minimal content. There is a strong opportunity to dominate long-tail search with:
- Suburb-specific landing pages
- Cleaning guides and price comparison content
- Blog content targeting "how much does X cleaning cost in Canberra"

### Gap 10: Transparent, Fixed-Price Models
Most companies quote hourly or require inspections. CleanService's fixed-price-per-sqm model is rare. Consumers increasingly want upfront, predictable pricing.
