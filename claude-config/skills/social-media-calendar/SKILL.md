# Social Media Calendar

## Overview

This skill creates structured social media content calendars that plan posts across multiple platforms. It balances content pillars, suggests optimal posting times, aligns organic content with paid campaign schedules, and outputs a complete calendar with dates, times, platforms, copy, hashtags, media types, and content categories.

## When to Use This Skill

- When planning a week, two weeks, or month of social media content
- When launching a new brand or product and need a content rollout plan
- When aligning organic social with a paid advertising campaign
- When a content team needs a structured posting schedule to follow
- When diversifying content mix across educational, promotional, and engagement posts
- When expanding to a new social platform and need a tailored content plan

## How It Works

The skill takes brand information, campaign goals, content pillars, and time period requirements, then generates a complete posting calendar. It applies platform-specific best practices for posting frequency and timing, ensures content pillar balance, and produces ready-to-execute content briefs for each post.

## Instructions

### Input Requirements

Provide as many of the following as possible:

- **Brand/Business**: What the brand does, sells, or represents
- **Target Audience**: Who the content is for
- **Platforms**: Which platforms to plan for (Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Facebook)
- **Time Period**: How many days/weeks to plan (e.g., 1 week, 2 weeks, 1 month)
- **Content Pillars**: Categories of content (or let the skill suggest defaults)
- **Campaign Tie-ins**: Any ongoing paid campaigns, launches, or promotions to align with
- **Brand Voice**: Tone and style guidelines
- **Existing Assets**: Any photos, videos, or templates available
- **Key Dates**: Holidays, events, product launches, or industry dates within the period

### Platform-Specific Guidelines

**Instagram**
- Carousel posts get highest engagement; lead with a hook slide
- Reels should be 15-60 seconds with text overlays and trending audio
- Use 20-30 hashtags mixing popular, niche, and branded
- Stories: use polls, questions, and slider stickers for engagement

**TikTok**
- First 3 seconds must hook the viewer
- Native, raw-feeling content outperforms polished ads
- Participate in trending sounds and formats when relevant
- Post consistently; the algorithm rewards frequency

**LinkedIn**
- Personal stories and lessons outperform corporate announcements
- Use line breaks and short paragraphs for readability
- Documents/carousels (PDF uploads) get strong organic reach
- Tag relevant people and companies to extend reach

**X/Twitter**
- Threads outperform single tweets for educational content
- Engage with replies and quote tweets for visibility
- Use no more than 2-3 hashtags per tweet
- Images and videos increase engagement by 2-3x

**Facebook**
- Video content (especially live) gets priority in the feed
- Groups and community content outperform page posts
- Share links with custom preview text rather than auto-generated
- Boost top-performing organic posts as paid content

### Aligning Organic with Paid Campaigns

When a paid campaign is running:
1. **Pre-launch (1 week before):** Build anticipation with teaser organic content
2. **Launch day:** Coordinate organic posts across all platforms to amplify the paid message
3. **During campaign:** Support paid themes with organic social proof, UGC, and behind-the-scenes content
4. **Post-campaign:** Share results, testimonials, and follow-up content organically

### Best Practices

- Batch-create content for the week or month to maintain consistency
- Repurpose content across platforms by adapting format and tone (not copy-pasting)
- Leave 10-20% of calendar slots flexible for timely or reactive content
- Track performance weekly and adjust the next week's plan based on what worked
- Maintain a content bank of evergreen posts that can fill gaps
- Schedule posts using a social media management tool but monitor for real-time engagement
- Always review the calendar against upcoming holidays and events to avoid tone-deaf posting
