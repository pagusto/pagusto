# Ad Copywriter

## Overview

This skill generates high-performing ad copy tailored to specific advertising platforms and formats. It respects platform-specific character limits, applies proven copywriting frameworks, and produces multiple variations suitable for A/B testing. The output adapts tone and messaging based on the brand voice, target audience, and campaign objective.

## When to Use This Skill

- When creating new ad campaigns across one or multiple platforms
- When refreshing stale ad creative with new copy variations
- When A/B testing headlines, descriptions, or CTAs
- When adapting a single campaign message across different platforms
- When a client needs ad copy that follows platform best practices
- When scaling ad production and need multiple variations quickly

## How It Works

The skill takes campaign parameters (product/service, audience, objective, platform, brand voice) and generates copy that follows platform-specific constraints and proven direct response copywriting principles. It applies frameworks like AIDA (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action), PAS (Problem, Agitate, Solution), and BAB (Before, After, Bridge) to structure compelling messages.

## Instructions

### Input Requirements

Provide as many of the following as possible:
- **Product/Service**: What is being advertised
- **Target Audience**: Demographics, psychographics, pain points
- **Platform**: Meta (Facebook/Instagram), Google Ads, TikTok, LinkedIn, YouTube, Microsoft Ads
- **Campaign Objective**: Awareness, consideration, conversion, retargeting
- **Brand Voice**: Professional, casual, playful, authoritative, empathetic, bold
- **Key Benefits/USPs**: What differentiates the product
- **Offer/Promotion**: Any specific deal, discount, or limited-time offer
- **CTA Preference**: Sign up, shop now, learn more, get started, book a demo, etc.

### Platform Character Limits Reference

#### Meta (Facebook/Instagram)
- **Primary Text**: 125 characters (recommended, up to 2200 visible)
- **Headline**: 40 characters (recommended, max 255)
- **Description**: 30 characters (recommended, max 255)
- **CTA**: Platform-provided buttons (Shop Now, Learn More, Sign Up, etc.)

#### Google Ads (Search)
- **Headlines**: 30 characters each (up to 15 headlines)
- **Descriptions**: 90 characters each (up to 4 descriptions)
- **Display Path**: 15 characters per segment (2 segments)

#### Google Ads (Performance Max / Display)
- **Short Headline**: 30 characters (up to 5)
- **Long Headline**: 90 characters (up to 5)
- **Description**: 90 characters (up to 5)

#### TikTok
- **Ad Text**: 100 characters (recommended, max 999)
- **Display Name**: 40 characters
- Keep copy conversational, native-feeling, and trend-aware

#### LinkedIn
- **Introductory Text**: 150 characters (recommended, up to 600)
- **Headline**: 70 characters (recommended, max 200)
- **Description**: 100 characters (recommended, max 300)

#### YouTube
- **In-stream Headline**: 15 characters
- **In-stream Description**: 2 lines, 35 characters each
- **Discovery Headline**: 100 characters
- **Discovery Description**: 2 lines, 35 characters each

#### Microsoft Ads
- **Headlines**: 30 characters each (up to 15)
- **Descriptions**: 90 characters each (up to 4)
- Same structure as Google Ads Search

### Generating Ad Copy

1. **Analyze the brief** and identify the strongest angle (pain point, benefit, social proof, urgency, curiosity).
2. **Select a copywriting framework** appropriate to the objective:
   - Awareness: AIDA, storytelling hooks, curiosity gaps
   - Consideration: PAS, social proof, comparison
   - Conversion: Urgency, scarcity, direct benefit, risk reversal
   - Retargeting: Reminder, objection handling, incentive
3. **Write 3-5 variations** per ad component (headline, description, primary text).
4. **Include hooks** in the first line to stop the scroll: questions, bold claims, numbers, "you" statements, pattern interrupts.
5. **Apply emotional triggers** where appropriate: fear of missing out, aspiration, belonging, curiosity, trust.
6. **Add urgency elements** when relevant: limited time, limited quantity, deadline, exclusive access.

### Best Practices

- Always front-load the most important message in the first few words
- Use numbers and specifics over vague claims ("Save 47%" vs "Save money")
- Include the target keyword in Google/Microsoft Ads headlines for Quality Score
- For Meta, write primary text that works both truncated (125 chars) and expanded
- For TikTok, use informal language and avoid sounding like a traditional ad
- For LinkedIn, maintain professional tone but avoid jargon-heavy corporate speak
- Never use ALL CAPS for entire sentences; use it sparingly for one or two words of emphasis
- Include power words: free, new, proven, guaranteed, instant, exclusive, limited, secret
- Test different CTA buttons; sometimes "Learn More" outperforms "Shop Now" for cold audiences
- When writing multiple variations, vary the angle significantly, not just word substitutions
