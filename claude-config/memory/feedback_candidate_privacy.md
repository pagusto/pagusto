When creating or presenting candidate profiles, always:
- HIDE: email address and phone number
- HIDE: personal references and referee details
- KEEP: location/suburb/city (e.g., Brisbane QLD, Logan QLD)

**Why:** Candidate privacy — contact details should not be exposed in documents shared with clients until the candidate is confirmed for interview.

When formatting CVs, use bold sparingly — only on: candidate name, section headings, and job titles. Do NOT bold company names, dates, bullet points, or licence names. Less is more.

**Why:** User explicitly corrected over-bolding — wants bold only on truly important elements, not everything.

**How to apply:** Any time a candidate CV or profile is created, formatted, or presented — strip email, phone, and references before outputting or saving.
