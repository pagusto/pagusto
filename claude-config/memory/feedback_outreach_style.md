Paul likes Chris Voss (FBI negotiation) techniques applied to recruitment outreach:
- **"Is it a bad time to talk?"** / **"Would it be a bad idea to..."** — no-oriented questions that almost always get a positive response
- Short, casual emails (5 lines max) — reads in 10 seconds
- Friendly Aussie tone ("mate", "ripper", "no stress")
- Subject lines that create curiosity, not sound like spam ("Quick question, [Name]")

**Why:** Paul has a Latino accent and engineering background — the Voss technique helps him sound natural and non-pushy on calls. He specifically referenced Peter Voss (FBI) approach.

**How to apply:** All outreach templates (email, SMS, phone scripts) should use Voss-style no-oriented openers and keep it brief and friendly. Never sound corporate or salesy.
